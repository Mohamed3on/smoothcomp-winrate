Mat Stats recounts every record at a Smoothcomp event from the event's own matches, and adds the views the site doesn't have.

Smoothcomp's win rate counts a walkover as a win, so an athlete who advanced on two no-shows looks the same as one who submitted two opponents. Mat Stats reads every match off the event's schedule, live or finished, and counts only the win types you choose: submissions, stoppages, points, decisions and DQs, with walkovers off unless you switch them on.

RESULTS PAGE
• Athletes: wins and losses, W–L, win rate, how each athlete won and lost, medals, and the size of their biggest bracket
• Every athlete's matches under their divisions, including the ones still to come, with a mark on anyone who still has a match to fight
• Academies: every academy's record, how its wins and losses came, its medals, and each athlete's matches
• Brackets: each bracket's size and gold medallist, beside whoever won the most matches there; open one for its standings and matches
• Matchups: any two academies head to head, with the record between them and every match where they met
• Belt and age filters, a minimum match count, and search across names, academies, divisions and countries
• Each athlete's record inline in the official results, which you can reorder by placement, bracket size or wins
• Divisions still waiting on published results count from the schedule, with medals read off their final and bronze matches

PARTICIPANTS PAGE
• Every bracket at once, biggest first, instead of a few at a time as you scroll, including athletes still waiting on approval
• Each athlete's career beside their name: wins, how many came by submission and on points, and medals
• Each bracket ranked by those careers, so you can size up your division before you compete, or the brackets sorted by how stacked they are
• Athletes: everyone at the event in one list, ranked by career wins or submission wins, with their belt, age, divisions and the medal each bracket points to
• Academies: every academy ranked by its athletes' combined career wins, with the athletes behind it and the medals they're on course for
• Belt, age, country and approved-only filters
• Signed in: every bracket you could enter and would top-seed, with the athlete you'd displace

Works on smoothcomp.com events and on AJP Tour events (ajptour.com).

PRIVACY
Mat Stats runs only on Smoothcomp and AJP Tour event pages. It reads what those sites already show you, keeps a cache in your browser so repeat visits make fewer requests, and sends nothing anywhere else: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Mat Stats is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Smoothcomp or AJP Tour.
