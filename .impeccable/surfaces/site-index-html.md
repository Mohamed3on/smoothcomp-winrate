# Mat Stats public site

Mode: Operate. Audience: competitors, coaches, academy owners checking rankings, an athlete, a team or an event outside Smoothcomp. Data: the shared D1 cache the extension fills.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One pooled ranking you can bend with the extension's own filters — finish types counted, belts, minimum wins and events, country — then follow any name to its page. Refuses the dashboard of tiles and mini-leaderboards.
OWN-WORLD: The extension's dark ledger (DESIGN.md dark set): #111316 page, #17191c surface, #1e2126 raised bands, one Signal Blue accent #79b3ea, the win-type ramp as the only data colour, medal colours on podium counts, belt swatches. Flat, 1px rules, tabular numerals, system sans.
STORY: The visitor lands on the athlete ranking, tightens the filters until the sample is trustworthy, and reads why each athlete ranks (finish mix bar, medals, wins per event). From there: academies ranked with their top athletes, events with podiums and entrants.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (wordmark, Athletes / Academies / Events tabs, global search, site select). Under it a filter band: finish-type chips, belt chips, minimum wins, minimum events, country. Then the ranked table filling the width, sortable headers, the win-type ramp bar per row.
FORM: user-steered rankings-first ledger, from the re-roll steer. seed: none (pinned) — "i want rankings in general, for ex atheletes pooled in 1 ranking with filters like sorting by wins, win types, al lthe filters we have rn in extension. same with academies, wins, wins by athletes, top athletes in an academy etc. event results and participants also cool"
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
